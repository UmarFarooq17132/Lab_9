Why Scaling Belongs Inside the Pipeline:Prevents Data Leakage: During cross-validation, StandardScaler computes the mean and standard deviation only on the 4 training folds before transforming the validation fold. Scaling before GridSearchCV leaks validation distribution statistics into training.Fair Regularization: Ridge penalizes the sum of squared coefficients ($\alpha \sum b_j^2$). Polynomial expansion creates terms on vastly different numerical scales (e.g., $x^3$ vs. $x$); scaling puts all features on unit variance so Ridge shrinks them fairly.
